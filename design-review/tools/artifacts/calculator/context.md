# Support cost calculator

A small calculator the support leads use during quarterly planning to estimate
the monthly cost of ticket handling. It multiplies ticket volume by handling
time and a loaded hourly rate.

## Assumptions

- Handling time is the median from the help desk export for September 2026.
- The loaded hourly rate includes salary, benefits and tooling, not management.

- Should escalated tickets use a separate handling time?
